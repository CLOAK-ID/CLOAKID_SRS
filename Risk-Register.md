# CLOAKID — Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies potential risks that could affect the CLOAKID project's scope, schedule, documentation, implementation, security, or collaboration.

The risks listed below are initial planning risks, not confirmed problems. The team should review them and update their likelihood, impact, and status as more project information becomes available.

## 2. Risk Rating

**Likelihood**
- Low: Unlikely to occur.
- Medium: Could occur during the project.
- High: Likely to occur without preventive action.

**Impact**
- Low: Limited effect on project progress or quality.
- Medium: Noticeable rework, delay, or quality reduction.
- High: Significant delay, major rework, or failure to meet an important requirement.

## 3. Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Requirements are unclear or incomplete. | Medium | High | Review the SRS early, document ambiguities, and agree on clarifications before implementation. |
| R02 | Project scope expands beyond the approved requirements. | Medium | High | Compare proposed features against the SRS and document any approved scope changes. |
| R03 | Project milestones are delayed. | Medium | High | Break work into smaller tasks, monitor progress, and identify blockers early. |
| R04 | Team responsibilities overlap or important tasks have no owner. | Medium | Medium | Assign a responsible person to each task and clarify dependencies. |
| R05 | Git merge conflicts or accidental changes affect project files. | Medium | Medium | Work on separate branches, commit regularly, review diffs, and merge changes carefully. |
| R06 | Documentation becomes inconsistent with the SRS or implementation. | Medium | High | Review related documents whenever requirements or technical decisions change. |
| R07 | Security or privacy requirements are overlooked. | Medium | High | Review relevant requirements during design and testing, and document safeguards and unresolved concerns. |
| R08 | Implementation defects remain undiscovered. | Medium | High | Prepare requirement-based tests, record defects, and retest after fixes. |
| R09 | Unverified assumptions are treated as confirmed system features. | Medium | Medium | Clearly distinguish approved requirements, proposed designs, and future enhancements. |
| R10 | Important files are misplaced, duplicated, or overwritten. | Medium | Medium | Maintain a clear repository structure, review file changes, and use version control history. |

## 4. Risk Response and Monitoring

The team should review this register during project progress reviews and whenever a significant change occurs.

For each risk, the team should:

1. Check whether the risk has occurred or become more likely.
2. Assess its effect on scope, schedule, quality, or security.
3. Apply the listed mitigation or agree on an additional response.
4. Assign an owner where follow-up is required.
5. Record the current status and any decisions.

## 5. Risk Status

Use the following status values:

- **Open:** The risk has been identified and requires monitoring.
- **Mitigating:** Actions are being taken to reduce the risk.
- **Occurred:** The risk has become an actual issue requiring action.
- **Closed:** The risk no longer requires active monitoring.
