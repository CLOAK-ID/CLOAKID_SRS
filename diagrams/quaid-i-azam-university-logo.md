## Description
Official emblem of **Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad**, in green on a white background: a crescent and star above an open book, surrounded by a floral wreath, with a ribbon reading "QUAID-I-AZAM UNIVERSITY" and "ISLAMABAD" below.

## Usage
Used on the project's title/cover page as the institution logo. It is not a system diagram.
